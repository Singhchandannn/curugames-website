PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, explained clearly.

This page explains the main categories of information used by Curu Games and connected Curu services.

1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how Curu Games and The Curu Shop handle information when you use the Curu Games Android app, this website and connected Curu services.

2. Information you provide

Depending on the feature you use, information may include account details, display name, support messages, referral information and other information you choose to submit.

3. Sign-in and account information

Curu Games can use Google sign-in and Curu account authentication. Authentication tokens are used to establish your session. The app does not need your Google account password.

4. Gameplay, rewards and device information

The service may process gameplay events, reward eligibility, CURU Points, conversion activity, device identifiers or integrity signals needed for security, anti-abuse, account/device rules and service operation.

5. Advertising and consent

The Android app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform where applicable. Ad delivery and consent behavior can depend on region, consent status, device settings and Google’s services.

6. Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used to deliver app notifications. You can control notification permissions through Android settings.

7. Parent Control and Kids Mode

Parent Control settings such as the local Parent PIN, selected games and Kids Mode state are designed to stay on the device in the current app implementation. Kids Mode does not itself create a separate child account.

8. How information is used

  • Operate the game and account experience.
  • Track eligible gameplay and reward activity.
  • Protect the service against abuse or unauthorized activity.
  • Provide support and notifications.
  • Improve reliability and user experience.

9. Account deletion

Use the in-app account deletion option or follow the steps on the Account Deletion page.

10. Contact

Questions can be sent to thecurushop@gmail.com.
