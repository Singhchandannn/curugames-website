WHY CURU GAMES

Games are fun. Curu gives the session a next step.

Many mobile games are designed only around the time you spend on the screen. Curu Games keeps the entertainment, then adds a reward path that can connect eligible play to physical 3D printed products.

Entertainment first

Curu Games is still a game app. Built-in and Pro Games are there because users want quick, varied things to play.

Eligible play can build CURU Points

Instead of treating gameplay and the Curu Shop as completely separate experiences, eligible active play can earn CURU Points according to current live rules.

Store Credit can go beyond a small coupon

Eligible Points can convert to Curu Store Credit. When the eligible balance is sufficient, that credit can cover up to 100% of an eligible order amount under the live checkout rules.

A physical outcome makes the idea easier to understand

3D printing is tangible. A toy, figurine, organizer, nameplate or decorative piece is something a user can actually hold. That is why the Curu ecosystem connects gaming rewards with a 3D printing store instead of only showing another in-app badge.

Not a claim that every other game gives nothing

Different apps have different reward systems. Curu Games' specific approach is the combination of games, eligible CURU Points, Store Credit and The Curu Shop's 3D printed product ecosystem.

Designed for families too

Parent Control adds game selection, PIN/biometric unlock and Kids Mode so a parent can shape the visible catalog without removing the basic game experience.
