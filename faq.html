FAQ

Questions about Curu Games, answered clearly.

Find quick answers about games, rewards, 3D printing, Parent Control, account management and Google Play.

What is Curu Games?

Curu Games is an Android game app from The Curu Shop with built-in games, an available Pro Games catalog, CURU Points, Store Credit integration and Parent Control.

How do CURU Points work?

Eligible active online play can earn CURU Points according to the current live rules shown inside the app. Rates and eligibility can change.

Can Store Credit cover a full order?

When your eligible Curu Store Credit balance is sufficient, it can cover up to 100% of the eligible checkout amount, subject to the live checkout rules and any exclusions or additional charges.

Are CURU Points cash?

No. CURU Points are an in-app reward unit and Curu Store Credit is intended for eligible purchases in the Curu ecosystem. They are not represented as guaranteed cash income.

What are Pro Games?

Pro Games are additional games delivered through the live Curu Games catalog when that feature is enabled and games are available.

What happens when Pro Games are turned off?

The public website hides the live Pro Games section when the catalog is unavailable or disabled. Built-in games remain part of the app.

How does Parent Control work?

A signed-in parent can select visible built-in and Pro Games, configure a Parent PIN, optionally use supported biometrics, and turn Kids Mode on or off.

Does uninstalling reset Parent Control?

The current design stores Parent Control locally and excludes it from Android backup/transfer, so uninstalling the app resets that local configuration.

How do I delete my account?

Use the in-app account deletion option or follow the instructions on the Account Deletion page. You can also contact thecurushop@gmail.com for help.

Where can I get Curu Games?

Use the official Google Play link on this website. The package is com.thecurushop.curugames.
