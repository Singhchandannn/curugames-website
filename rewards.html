REWARDS

What are CURU Points — and what can they become?

CURU Points are the in-app reward unit used by Curu Games. The app shows the live earning and conversion rules so users can understand what is currently eligible.

Eligible gameplay rewards

Eligible active online play earns CURU Points according to the current live rate.

Not every second the app is open necessarily counts. Eligibility can depend on active play, connection state, account state, device rules and anti-abuse checks.

From Points to Store Credit

The current conversion is always shown inside the app.

Eligible Points can convert into Curu Store Credit. Store Credit is designed for eligible purchases from The Curu Shop and is subject to the checkout rules shown at the time of use.

Up to 100% of an eligible order

This is not designed as a small percentage-off coupon. When the eligible Store Credit balance is sufficient, it can cover up to 100% of the eligible checkout amount. Other charges or exclusions may still apply according to the live checkout rules.

Why the app uses live rules

Reward rates, conversion values, eligibility limits and available products can change over time. Showing them from the live configuration helps keep the app aligned with the current Curu Games and Curu Shop experience.

Important reward boundaries

  • CURU Points and Store Credit are not represented as guaranteed cash income.
  • Reward eligibility can be limited by anti-abuse, device, account or campaign rules.
  • Pro Games availability can change.
  • Store Credit use follows the live checkout rules.

For the formal rules, read the Rewards Terms.
