ABOUT CURU GAMES

A game experience connected to The Curu Shop.

Curu Games brings together mobile games, eligible reward progression, family controls and The Curu Shop’s 3D printing ecosystem.

The idea

Gaming is a familiar way to spend short breaks and longer sessions. Curu Games adds a path from eligible play to CURU Points, from Points to Store Credit, and from Store Credit to eligible physical products.

The Curu Shop connection

The Curu Shop is the commerce side of the ecosystem. It provides the live product and checkout experience where eligible Curu Store Credit can be used.

What the app includes

  • Built-in games.
  • Available Pro Games from the live catalog.
  • CURU Points and Store Credit integration.
  • Google sign-in and account features.
  • Parent Control and Kids Mode.
  • Events, favourites, referrals and support features.

Contact

For support, account help or product questions, contact thecurushop@gmail.com.
